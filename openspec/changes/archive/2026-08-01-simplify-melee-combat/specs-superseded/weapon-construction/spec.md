## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Impact Strength From Muzzle Size
Impact Strength SHALL equal the size of the muzzle — or, for melee weapons, the functional striking end — that generated the attack (e.g. a 1×1 muzzle or striking end has size 1, a 2×2 has size 2, a 3×3 has size 3, a 4×4 has size 4).

#### Scenario: Impact strength computed from muzzle size
- **WHEN** a weapon has a 2×2 muzzle
- **THEN** the attack die generated by that muzzle has Impact Strength 2

#### Scenario: Mixed muzzle sizes produce independent impact strengths
- **WHEN** a weapon has one 2×2 muzzle and two 1×1 muzzles
- **THEN** it produces 3 Attack Dice with Impact Strengths 2, 1, and 1 respectively

#### Scenario: Impact strength computed from a melee weapon's striking end size
- **WHEN** a melee weapon has a 2×2 functional striking end
- **THEN** the Attack Die it generates has Impact Strength 2
