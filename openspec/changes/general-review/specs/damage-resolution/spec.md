# damage-resolution Specification Delta

Two requirements change owner. `16-damage-system.md` stated the Attack Roll
(`DMG-011`) and Weapon Distribution (`DMG-018`) alongside `11-combat.md`, which
stated both as well. **The duplicates are removed from the damage document and
`11-combat.md` keeps them** — the attacker's side of combat belongs to the
combat document, and neither mechanic changes.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Attack Roll
For every Attack Die generated by a weapon (per `10-weapons.md` WPN-006 for ranged weapons and `12-melee.md` MEL-003 for melee weapons), the attacker SHALL roll one D6. A result of 4, 5, or 6 SHALL create one valid Impact. A result of 1, 2, or 3 SHALL cause that die to produce no Impact. Where the component providing the attack is `Wounded` — the weapon, or the attacker itself in an unarmed attack — the attacker SHALL roll two D6 for that die and SHALL read only the lower of the two against those same results. The number of Attack Dice rolled SHALL NOT change with any component's state, and the second die SHALL NOT be an Impact of its own. This SHALL be stated once, by `11-combat.md`, and the damage document SHALL NOT restate it.

#### Scenario: Successful attack roll creates a valid impact
- **WHEN** an attack roll for a generated Impact results in 4, 5, or 6
- **THEN** that Impact is valid and proceeds to target component selection

#### Scenario: Failed attack roll removes the impact
- **WHEN** an attack roll for a generated Impact results in 1, 2, or 3
- **THEN** that Impact has no further effect

#### Scenario: A Wounded weapon rolls two dice and reads the lower
- **WHEN** an attack roll is made for an Impact generated by a `Wounded` weapon
- **THEN** two D6 are rolled and only the lower result is read, so the Impact is valid only when both dice show 4, 5, or 6

#### Scenario: The extra die is not an extra Impact
- **WHEN** a `Wounded` weapon with one functional muzzle attacks
- **THEN** it rolls two dice and can produce at most one Impact, exactly as it could while `Operational`

#### Scenario: An unarmed attacker's own state is read
- **WHEN** a `Wounded` minifigure makes an unarmed attack, where the attacker is its own weapon system
- **THEN** its single Attack Die is rolled as two D6 with only the lower read, the same as for a `Wounded` weapon

### Requirement: Weapon Distribution
By default, all Impacts generated by the same weapon system SHALL be assigned to the same target unit. A weapon mounted on a free-rotating mount (e.g. turntable, ball joint, swivel mount) SHALL be permitted to assign its Impacts to different target units instead. A weapon on a fixed mount SHALL NOT split its Impacts across target units. This SHALL be stated once, by `11-combat.md`, and the damage document SHALL NOT restate it.

#### Scenario: Fixed-mount weapon cannot split impacts across targets
- **WHEN** a weapon system is mounted on a fixed mount and generates multiple Impacts
- **THEN** all of its Impacts are assigned to the same target unit

#### Scenario: Free-rotating mount may split impacts across targets
- **WHEN** a weapon system is mounted on a free-rotating mount (turntable, ball joint, or swivel mount) and generates multiple Impacts
- **THEN** its Impacts may be assigned to different target units
