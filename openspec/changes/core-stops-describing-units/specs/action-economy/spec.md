## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Action Cost Does Not Scale With Size
An action's Action Point cost SHALL NOT scale with the size of the unit performing it — its footprint, its height, or the number of Unit Bases it occupies — nor with the size of an element that action operates. Where more than one Action Point is spent, the reason SHALL be stated in the rule that spends it and SHALL NOT be size. A measurement MAY decide which rule applies to an action; it SHALL NOT decide what that action costs.

**Reason for removal:** the requirement states a prohibition against a pricing scheme no rule uses. Every action in the ruleset carries a flat cost written into the rule that charges it — 1 Action Point to move, to rotate, to attack per weapon system, to open any interactive element, to repair, to embark, to disembark — and the two rules that once priced by Unit Base were rewritten flat by the change that added this requirement. What remained was a ban on a thing the ruleset no longer does, restated in `CORE-006` and cited by the two rules it had corrected.

`TRN-005` and `TRN-006` keep stating their own flat cost; they simply stop deferring it to a rule about size. The behaviour every scenario below describes is unchanged and is still what the ruleset produces — an infantry model and a four-Unit-Base unit both spend 1 Action Point to embark, because `TRN-005` says 1 Action Point and says nothing about Unit Bases.

**What still guards it:** `CORE-006` fixes the allotment at 3 Action Points with no way to earn more, and every rule that charges states its own cost. A price that grew with the model would have to be written into one of those rules, where it would be visible against the fixed budget rather than smuggled past a general prohibition.
